| :------- | :--------: |

| Edit Flashcards | E |

1. New Cards

   - Graduating Interval (15 days)

   - Easy interval (60 days)
2. Reviews
   - Maximum Cards (9999)
3. Lapses
   - New Interval (70%)
   - Leech Threshold (8)
   - Tag ONLY
